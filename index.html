A working archive for the Northern Tujia Language: dictionary, spelling, grammar and texts.

Southern and unclassified source records are kept as clearly labelled reference material.

Dictionary Search by Tujia spelling, source transcription, English or Chinese. → Spelling System Reference tables for 21 initials, 23 finals and four basic tones. → Grammar An introductory reference to Northern Tujia Language word classes, verb marking and syntax. → Texts A growing collection of Northern Tujia Language stories, conversations, songs and audio from multiple sources. →

Dialect note. The draft spelling scheme is based on the Northern Tujia Language, with Miaoshi in Longshan as its reference point. Southern source forms remain visible in IPA, and their converted spellings are marked as reference forms.
