Cultural note · 文化注释

A comic cautionary tale about a father and his literal-minded son. The son's mechanical imitation—spilling oil and copying his father's mishap with noodles—shows the danger of following instructions without understanding context.

这是一篇带有劝诫意味的笑话故事。儿子机械模仿父亲，把桐油倒掉，又模仿父亲把面条弄进鼻子，说明只会照做而不懂情境会闹出笑话。
