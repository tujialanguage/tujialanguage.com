3.3.1: Village Life3.3.1: 村里的生活

These two texts paint starkly contrastive pictures of daily life. At first glance it might seem as though women do all the work, but this is not really the case. Educating children costs money, so typically the men go into the big cities to work in factories or on building sites while their wives manage the family farm on their own. Once their children have left home, life becomes a little more relaxed. Older folk often just look after the livestock while younger family members farm the land.

SourceNian3qing1 be2 hong3huan4 hang2nie3, be2 gai4-lang2 ge2-bo3-da2 la, nga2 ni2 qi1ma1 si3 lai1 nian3, si3 lai1 nian3 ci4.

ZH年轻的时候也是这样。我们的年龄也没有隔多少，你起码比我大十来年，十来年。

DraftA: aix sir, sir yif nianr civ-nier, ngaf longrbair qif sir suif-liaor wa. sef me, sangx sif. B: ngaf me, sif sir luf qif-liaor. A: jiux jianx luo, sef davzer me hair zaif qingrnief.

ZHA：那大十、十一年，我今年七十岁了。你们呢，三四十。B：我四十六七了。A：就是嘛，像你们呢，还在后面。

DraftB: yex hangfnier la. aixdix youf hangfnier, suovtir bef hangfnier, yer rix bef yaf laoxhuov, hongrhuanv gaif davzer, nif guofduor kuv me rix-tax youf bangfhuax taif.

SourceB: ye1 hang2nie3 la. ai1di1 you2 hang2nie3, suo4ti3 be2 hang2nie3, ye3 ri1 be2 ya2 lao1huo4, hong3huan4 gai2 da4ze3, ni2 guo2duo3 ku4 me ri1-ta1 you2 bang2hua1 tai2.

ZHB：也是这样的。那又这样啊，身体也这样，做工也比较累。总是这样，你自己苦，不做又没有办法。

DraftA: aix huanf rix-duor, ziv la longv a, ziv gav longr longv me, yer rix me nif bef gev bor laf longx huirliaof le, bef tongrqianr...

SourceA: ai1 huan2 ri1-duo3, zi4 la long4 a, zi4 ga4 long3 long4 me, ye3 ri1 me ni2 be2 ge4 bo3 la2 long1 hui3liao2 le, be2 tong3qian3...

ENIt's always like this, you work hard, but you have no choice. A: You have to keep on working: raise some pigs, raise a few pigs, work in the fields, you give them fertiliser for a year, you need money...

ZHA：那总是要做的。养猪啊，养几只猪啊，做工呢，你给它们一年的肥料呢，也要钱。

DraftB: jiux huofnier bufsix, nif cux gaox bef suanf buf longx zangf, nif qiev gaox duor bef laoxhuov, youf yer rix, qiev gaox duor bef hangfnier.

SourceB: jiu1 huo2nie3 bu2si1, ni2 cu1 gao1 be2 suan2 bu2 long1 zang2, ni2 qie4 gao1 duo3 be2 lao1huo4, you2 ye3 ri1, qie4 gao1 duo3 be2 hang2nie3.

ENB: It's like that, isn't it; you can't make ends meet, whatever you do leaves you tired, you work some more, whatever you do is like this.

ZHB：就是这样嘛，你到家也算不拢帐，你搞什么都累。又做工，搞什么也都是这样的。

DraftA: youf baixdiex muflax cixcix tux-nier sirjier me, ziv laov niex longr longv a, gevzex laov congx heng, jiux huofnier, bef tongrqianr dianf xief, cixcix lax pangr.

SourceA: you2 bai1die1 mu2la1 ci1ci1 tu1-nie3 si3jie3 me, zi4 lao4 nie1 long3 long4 a, ge4ze1 lao4 cong1 heng, jiu1 huo2nie3, be2 tong3qian3 dian2 xie2, ci1ci1 la1 pang3.

ENA: Now my child is at school, when they're at school, I keep two pigs, enough for school fees. In this way, I can have some money to send them to school.

ZHA：现在孩子又在读书，读书的时候，养一两只猪，充当他们的学费。就这样，钱也有一点，供一下他们读书。

DraftB: gaif gaox-tax me keir-liex huor-dif o? nif yerrixmax toursengx-bor bangfhuax taif, gaox-tax me, hangfnier.

SourceB: gai2 gao1-ta1 me kei3-lie1 huo3-di2 o? ni2 ye3ri1ma1 tou3seng1-bo3 bang2hua1 tai2, gao1-ta1 me, hang2nie3.

ENB: If you don't do this, where would you get [money] from? If farming is your lot, that's the only way to get it.

ZHB：不搞这个的话，钱从哪里来呢？你投生了农民，不搞没有办法，是这样的。

DraftA: yerrixmax me, huanf zangvjingr, huanf guofduor jifhuax laf div o.

SourceA: ye3ri1ma1 me, huan2 zang4jing3, huan2 guo2duo3 ji2hua1 la2 di4 o.

ENA: Farmers, they're getting ahead, but they must have their own plans.

ZHA：农民呢，总是要长进，总是自己要有个计划。

DraftB: eng, gaox-tax me bufxingr, nif gaox-tax me taif, hangfnier dianf duor taif, hangflar, ang nif gaox-cex me, bef dianf xief ang, hangflar ang...

SourceB: eng, gao1-ta1 me bu2xing3, ni2 gao1-ta1 me tai2, hang2nie3 dian2 duo3 tai2, hang2la3, ang ni2 gao1-ce1 me, be2 dian2 xie2 ang, hang2la3 ang...

ENB: If you don't, it won't do; if you don't, then you can't [earn money], you won't have anything. If you do something, then you'll get a bit, it's like that...

ZHB：哼！不搞不行，你不搞就没有，这样一点都没有了，这样，你搞得呢，也有一点，就是这样。

DraftA: nif guofduor luov laf zif-bor, laordongflir me huanf dianf xief. B: sirtief ngangf-daf-nief, mefzer keir-liex huor-dif o? qiev duor taif la, aix sirjier davzer, cux gaox. A: nif aixdix rix hengv-max jiaxcux me youf taif luo.

SourceA: ni2 guo2duo3 luo4 la2 zi2-bo3, lao3dong2li3 me huan2 dian2 xie2. B: si3tie2 ngang2-da2-nie2, me2ze3 kei3-lie1 huo3-di2 o? qie4 duo3 tai2 la, ai1 si3jie3 da4ze3, cu1 gao1. A: ni2 ai1di1 ri1 heng4-ma1 jia1cu1 me you2 tai2 luo.

ENA: You're also an able-bodied individual, you do have the ability to work. B: There's no water in the paddy fields, where are you going to get rain from? There's nothing worth selling, so we stayed at home, just like in the past. A: You don't have any fit young relatives who can help you.

ZHA：你自己就是一个人，劳动力你总是有点。B：田里没有水，雨从哪里来啊？什么都没有，那时候一样，在家做。A：你又没有那个做得厉害的家属。

DraftA: ngaf le zaovguxdiex le, eixdix dafkaix baf dianxzongx yixqianr zuf-diuf, zuf-le yixhouf le cer lianx tongx dixdix, cer dixdix yixhouf le, aixliex jiux xiexpongx laf dongf wox

ENA: In the morning, I usually get up before eight o'clock. After getting up, I fetch two buckets of water. After getting the water, I wash the cooking-pot.

ZHA：我呢，早晨呢，大概八点钟以前起来。起了以后呢，抬两桶水。抬水以后呢，然后就洗一下鼎锅。

DraftXiexpongx wox-le yixhouf le, zirhher wox-lier jiux aix meirlurzir gar-liex jiux mangxmangx lax bangf, gerlaxbix bangf le engxgexlaxbir haxcex youf dianf laf dongf zunxbeir, aixliex tuortuor [huorlier] guof cairmox laf dongf suov,

SourceXie1pong1 wo1-le yi1hou2 le, zi3hhe3 wo1-lie3 jiu1 ai1 mei3lu3zi3 ga3-lie1 jiu1 mang1mang1 la1 bang2, ge3la1bi1 bang2 le eng1ge1la1bi3 ha1ce1 you2 dian2 la2 dong2 zun1bei3, ai1lie1 tuo3tuo3 [huo3lie3] guo2 cai3mo1 la2 dong2 suo4,

DraftSuov-lier yixhouf eng, aixliex ngaf jiux zaovhuanr laf hur-langf gaf-lax-hur. ref huanf dixdix-bor hur-lax, ngaf huanf dafkaix ongx wor zef hairsif hur-duor.

ZH切了以后，然后我就一个人开始吃早饭。总是买酒喝，每餐五六口酒，我还是要喝的。

ZH我可以喝六口，六口还可以，体质还是好像还可以。嘿，今天没有事情，吃完早饭就洗一下碗。
