01

Dongdongkui咚咚喹

DraftDunr dunr kuix eir, dunr dunr kuix eir. yiv suer gaix sar geir ceir yiaov. baov jir gaix sar rar har jiaov. lix lix lax lar, dunr dunr kuiv eir. lunr tur gaiv sar ceir hur jiaov aor. jix dunr pox lier bar gur jiaov aor. dunr dunr kuiv eir, dunr dunr kuiv eir. bax lier dunr, bax lier car cir siv lier wunr. wunr liex gov dor wunr. rar lier sox bux taf box bier. rar bar laox jiex buaf liex bier. laof zix mif niex gax gir bar bux bax dir hur. wor tur af six siv lar biv ar af six wuav dir hur. hav lier biav cuaf dir hur. sav mir zir gar rar rar lier huf nix keiãf dix hur. ziv jir paf wuav dix hur.

ENDongdongkui, dongdongkui. We went to Yongshun to play and to Baojing to kick shuttlecocks. We went to Longtou to drink water and climbed the slope at Jingdong. The Balie daughter-in-law is in postpartum confinement: first fry three eggs, then kill a rooster. Her maternal grandparents will visit, bringing a cradle, quilt, puppy, rice sweets, chicken, eggs and a pork leg.

ZH咚咚喹，咚咚喹。到永顺街上玩去了，到保靖街上踢毽子去了。哩哩拉拉咚咚喹，到隆头街上喝水去了，到井洞坡爬坡去了。咚咚喹，巴列媳子坐月子。她自己坐，先煎三个鸡蛋，再杀一个公鸡。明后天外公外婆要来看望，送摇篮、送被子，小狗要带来，糖糍、鸡、鸡蛋全都挑来，还有猪腿也要背来。

Cultural note · 文化注释

A children's rhyme built around the refrain ‘dongdongkui.’ Its journey through Yongshun, Baojing, Longtou and Jingdong anchors the song in familiar regional places.

这是一首以“咚咚喹”为衬词的童谣，歌词串联永顺、保靖、隆头、井洞等地方。

02

Fine weather and rain天晴下雨

DraftLaor wuar cir, meif ax zeir. tiex bix lax kux ax seir? ngax gox zof mor liv qier! tiaor zir lar kox bier. lanf kux lax ax bier. tuanr qianr dier liaor. wuf bix lif lax wof tux peir liaor.

IPA[lau²¹ wua²¹ tsʰɨ²¹, mei³⁵ a⁵⁵ tsei²¹. tʰie⁵⁵ pi⁵⁵ la⁵⁵ kʰu⁵⁵ a⁵⁵ sei²¹? ŋa⁵⁵ ko⁵⁵ tso³⁵ mo²¹ li⁵³ tɕʰie²¹! tʰiau²¹ tsɿ²¹ la²¹ kʰo⁵⁵ pie²¹. lã³⁵ kʰu⁵⁵ la⁵⁵ a⁵⁵ pie²¹. tʰuã²¹ tɕʰiã²¹ tie²¹ liau²¹. wu³⁵ pi⁵⁵ li³⁵ la⁵⁵ wo³⁵ tʰu⁵⁵ pʰei²¹ liau²¹.]

ENThe sun has come out; rain has fallen. Who is on the road opposite? Let me catch him and make him plough. Bend the cattle switch, join the yoke—but the connecting loop breaks, and the calf lies down in the furrow.

ZH出太阳了，下雨了。对面路上是谁呀？我把他捉过来耕地吧！先把赶牛的细棍子折弯，把牛轭连接起来；但团圈断了，小牛就在犁路里趴下了。

Cultural note · 文化注释

A playful work song in which changes between sunshine and rain frame scenes of ploughing, handling cattle and field labor.

这是一首带有劳动生活色彩的童谣，以天晴下雨为引子，写耕地、赶牛和田间劳作。

03

Rain is coming天快下雨

DraftMeif meif kuaif zeiar jiex. ceir lur liv lux kuaif doar jief. meif meif kuaif zeiar jiex. sunf bax laox gar kuaif doar jief. ar niex ar bax kar hux yier rix lur. zeir sir dev bar hor dax. kuaif jiex poax jier. meif meif kuaif zeir ar jiex. ceir lur liv lux kuaif doar jief. ceir pur dor mor manv bor. sunf lunx cax, sav lunr caf.

ENSky, sky, let rain fall quickly; let a downpour stream down, and let big fish follow the water. Mother and father have gone to work in the hills without rain capes or hats—come home from work quickly. Fill the pond with water so we can raise fish and ducks.

ZH老天老天快下雨，倾盆大雨下下来；老天老天快下雨，大鱼顺水流下来。父母上山做工去，没拿蓑衣和斗笠，快些收工回家来。老天老天快下雨，倾盆大雨下下来；快把塘里水流满，好养鱼来好养鸭。

Cultural note · 文化注释

A rain-calling song: heavy rain is asked to fall so parents can return from hillside work, ponds can fill and fish can thrive.

这是一首祈雨歌，希望大雨落下，让上山做工的父母回家，也让池塘蓄水、鱼儿生长。

04

The sound of forging iron打铁声

DraftDor tief dor, dor tief dor. jiex zox par pux keir bor zaor? wor sar wuav lur. suf kuv biav wuav lur. hav lier biav coaf lur. dor tief dor, dor tief dor. jiev zor par pux kar wov lur.

IPA[to²¹ tʰie³⁵ to²¹, to²¹ tʰie³⁵ to²¹. tɕie⁵⁵ tso⁵⁵ pʰa²¹ pʰu⁵⁵ kʰei²¹ po²¹ tsau²¹? wo²¹ sa²¹ wua⁵³ lu²¹. su³⁵ kʰu⁵³ pia⁵³ wua⁵³ lu²¹. xa⁵³ lie²¹ pia⁵³ tsʰoa³⁵ lu²¹. to²¹ tʰie³⁵ to²¹, to²¹ tʰie³⁵ to²¹. tɕie⁵³ tso²¹ pʰa²¹ pʰu⁵⁵ kʰa²¹ wo⁵³ lu²¹.]

ENHammer the iron, hammer the iron. Where has Grandfather Blacksmith gone? He carried away the backbasket and the small wood knife and led away the puppy. Hammer the iron—Grandfather Blacksmith went to carry firewood.

ZH打铁打，打铁打。铁匠爷爷哪里去了？背篓背走了，小柴刀也背走了，小狗牵走了。打铁打，打铁打，铁匠爷爷背柴去了。

Cultural note · 文化注释

The repeated syllables imitate a blacksmith's hammer. The verse turns the rhythm of forging iron and the blacksmith's movements into song.

反复的音节模拟铁匠打铁声，把锻铁的节奏和铁匠的行动编成歌谣。

05

Smoke, drift that way烟子烟子往那边熏

DraftKeif kar keif kar ex geix box kar. seir sir laf bux ex geix box lar. keif kar keif kar ex geix box kar. reir bir cof laix ex geix box zaor.

IPA[kʰei³⁵ kʰa²¹ kʰei³⁵ kʰa²¹ e⁵⁵ kei⁵⁵ po⁵⁵ kʰa²¹. sei²¹ sɿ²¹ la³⁵ pu⁵⁵ e⁵⁵ kei⁵⁵ po⁵⁵ la²¹. kʰei³⁵ kʰa²¹ kʰei³⁵ kʰa²¹ e⁵⁵ kei⁵⁵ po⁵⁵ kʰa²¹. zei²¹ pi²¹ tsʰɔ³⁵ lai⁵⁵ e⁵⁵ kei⁵⁵ po⁵⁵ tsau²¹.]

ENSmoke, smoke, drift that way; put a drop of oil over there. Smoke, smoke, drift that way; the little mouse has come out and gone that way.

ZH烟子烟子往那边熏，一滴油放那边。烟子烟子往那边熏，老鼠儿出来走那边。

Cultural note · 文化注释

This rhyme was commonly recited when hearth smoke blew toward a person; people believed that chanting it would make the smoke turn away.

这首儿歌多在火塘生火、烟熏人时念，据说念了烟就不会再熏人。

06

Ailolo哎罗罗

DraftEf lov lor, ef lov lor. ar kox biav bix nif tax zir. ar kox bov lir meif dix bor. ef lov lor, ef lov lor. ar kox wiir liaox wur kar dor. wur lar liaox, ar kox bov lir. cuf jix max rix dor tax. ef lov lor, ef lov lor. af dax biav bix nif zif tax dor. af dax biav bix nif meif dix bor. ef lov lor, ef lov lor. af dax wiir liaox mair ceir kur ceif dor. ceir kur ceif liaox mair hof tax dor. hof tax liex six bax af six lar dor. six bax af six nif bor daf bov.

IPA[e³⁵ lo⁵³ lo²¹, e³⁵ lo⁵³ lo²¹. a²¹ kʰo⁵⁵ pia⁵³ pi⁵⁵ ni³⁵ tʰa⁵⁵ tsɿ²¹. a²¹ kʰo⁵⁵ po⁵³ li²¹ mei³⁵ ti⁵⁵ po²¹. e³⁵ lo⁵³ lo²¹, e³⁵ lo⁵³ lo²¹. a²¹ kʰo⁵⁵ wii²¹ liau⁵⁵ wu²¹ kʰa²¹ to²¹. wu²¹ la²¹ liau⁵⁵, a²¹ kʰo⁵⁵ po⁵³ li²¹. tsʰu³⁵ tɕi⁵⁵ ma⁵⁵ zɿ⁵⁵ to²¹ tʰa⁵⁵. e³⁵ lo⁵³ lo²¹, e³⁵ lo⁵³ lo²¹. a³⁵ ta⁵⁵ pia⁵³ pi⁵⁵ ni³⁵ tsɿ³⁵ tʰa⁵⁵ to²¹. a³⁵ ta⁵⁵ pia⁵³ pi⁵⁵ ni³⁵ mei³⁵ ti⁵⁵ po²¹. e³⁵ lo⁵³ lo²¹, e³⁵ lo⁵³ lo²¹. a³⁵ ta⁵⁵ wii²¹ liau⁵⁵ mai²¹ tsʰei²¹ kʰu²¹ tsʰei³⁵ to²¹. tsʰei²¹ kʰu²¹ tsʰei³⁵ liau⁵⁵ mai²¹ xo³⁵ tʰa⁵⁵ to²¹. xo³⁵ tʰa⁵⁵ lie⁵⁵ sɿ⁵⁵ pa⁵⁵ a³⁵ sɿ⁵⁵ la²¹ to²¹. sɿ⁵⁵ pa⁵⁵ a³⁵ sɿ⁵⁵ ni³⁵ po²¹ ta³⁵ po⁵³.]

ENAilolo, ailolo. Little brother, do not cry; be good. When you grow up you will herd cattle: tend one and gain another, with no other household work. Little sister, do not cry; be good. When you grow up you will spin hemp, weave cloth, sew new clothes and wear them.

ZH哎罗罗，哎罗罗。小哥哥你不要哭，小哥哥要听话。哥哥长大要守牛，守一头牛就长一个。家里什么都不要做。哎罗罗，哎罗罗。小姐姐你不要哭，小姐姐要听话。姐姐长大要绩麻，绩麻以后要织布，织布以后缝新衣服，新衣服就给你穿。

Cultural note · 文化注释

These are two lullabies. The singer selects different verses according to the child's sex: the boy is imagined herding cattle, while the girl is imagined spinning hemp, weaving and making clothes.

这是两首摇篮曲，吟唱者根据孩子的性别选择内容：男孩长大守牛，女孩长大绩麻、织布、缝衣。

IPA[a³⁵ ta²¹ lie³⁵ ka²¹ tɕiou²¹ kʰa⁵⁵ pʰu⁵⁵ tʰa⁵³. ŋa³⁵ sa⁵⁵ tsʰo⁵³ nie²¹ li⁵³ xu²¹ ka²¹. ŋa³⁵ tsei³⁵ tɕiou³⁵ ka²¹ lai²¹ li³⁵ jiou³⁵ a⁵⁵. ŋa³⁵ kʰo⁵⁵ ni³⁵ tsʰo⁵³ nie²¹ li⁵³ xi²¹ ka²¹. ni³⁵ tsei³⁵ jiou³⁵ ka²¹ lai²¹ li³⁵ jiou³⁵ a⁵⁵. ŋa³⁵ ni³⁵ a²¹ kʰo⁵⁵ ma²¹ ni⁵⁵ zɿ³⁵ to²¹ ta⁵⁵ ma²¹. ni⁵⁵ tsei³⁵ ka²¹ tɕiou³⁵ ŋa³⁵ tɕiau³⁵ tɕie³⁵ yei²¹ sa²¹? a³⁵ ta⁵⁵ ni³⁵ ko⁵⁵ tɕiou²¹ tsei³⁵ mo²¹ pa³⁵. a³⁵ ta²¹ nie²¹ lo³⁵ pu²¹ lo⁵³ zɿ³⁵ ta⁵⁵. a³⁵ ni³⁵ ti³⁵ ka²¹ nie²¹ yi³⁵ ta⁵⁵ po²¹ tsa³⁵ ma²¹. lai⁵⁵ jia⁵⁵ ni³⁵ ŋa³⁵ tɕiou³⁵ lo⁵³ zɿ³⁵ tʰa⁵⁵. a²¹ kʰo⁵⁵ a²¹ kʰo⁵⁵ jiou³⁵ ŋa³⁵ ni⁵⁵ tɕie²¹. ni³⁵ a³⁵ ta⁵⁵ sa²¹ lia²¹ mei²¹ tʰa⁵³ tɕia²¹. ŋa³⁵ ni³⁵ a²¹ kʰo⁵⁵ ma⁵⁵ tɕʰie³⁵ tu⁵⁵ to²¹ mei²¹ wo²¹ tʰu²¹ tie³⁵ ma⁵⁵. a³⁵ ni⁵⁵ nie⁵⁵ yi⁵⁵ tɕʰie³⁵ tu⁵⁵ ta⁵⁵ xa²¹ jie⁵⁵ jio²¹?]

ENSister weaves upstairs while I dig behind your house; I am thirsty and hungry, but you sent me no bowl of rice. Brother digs behind the house, also thirsty and hungry—yet we do not know each other, so what is that to me? Sister, your eyes fail to recognize me: we loved each other before, but today you deny me. Brother, do not think my words are my heart; I think of you day and night—when will we be together?

ZH姐在楼上忙织花，我在你们屋后挖生土；我口又干、肚又饿，姐碗中饭没送我。哥在屋后挖土，你也口干肚饿。我们相互不认识，跟我有什么关系？姐姐，我告诉你：姐的眼睛不认人。我们以前就相好，今天你却不认人。哥哥哥哥叫声你，姐是有口没有心；我日也思来夜也想，两人何时到一起？

Cultural note · 文化注释

An antiphonal courtship song performed by a man and a woman. Teasing accusations give way to a declaration that both have long cared for each other.

这是一首男女对唱的情歌，双方先以埋怨、试探互相应答，最后表明彼此早已相恋。
