Elder sister weaves upstairs姐在楼上织花

DraftAf dar lief gar jiur kax pux tav. ngaf sax cov nier liv hur gar. ngaf zeif jiuf gar lair lif yiuf ax. ngaf kox nif cov nier liv hir gar. nif zeif yiuf gar lair lif yiuf ax. ngaf nif ar kox mar nix rif dor dax mar. nix zeif gar jiuf ngaf jiaof jief yeir sar? af dax nif gox jiur zeif mor baf. af dar nier lof bur lov rif dax. af nif dif gar nier yif dax bor zaf mar. laix yiax nif ngaf jiuf lov rif tax. ar kox ar kox yiuf ngaf nix jier. nif af dax sar liar meir tav jiar. ngaf nif ar kox max qief dux dor meir wor tur dief max. af nix niex yix qief dux dax har yiex yior?

ENSister weaves upstairs while I dig behind your house; I am thirsty and hungry, but you sent me no bowl of rice. Brother digs behind the house, also thirsty and hungry—yet we do not know each other, so what is that to me? Sister, your eyes fail to recognize me: we loved each other before, but today you deny me. Brother, do not think my words are my heart; I think of you day and night—when will we be together?

ZH姐在楼上忙织花，我在你们屋后挖生土；我口又干、肚又饿，姐碗中饭没送我。哥在屋后挖土，你也口干肚饿。我们相互不认识，跟我有什么关系？姐姐，我告诉你：姐的眼睛不认人。我们以前就相好，今天你却不认人。哥哥哥哥叫声你，姐是有口没有心；我日也思来夜也想，两人何时到一起？

Cultural note · 文化注释

An antiphonal courtship song performed by a man and a woman. Teasing accusations give way to a declaration that both have long cared for each other.

这是一首男女对唱的情歌，双方先以埋怨、试探互相应答，最后表明彼此早已相恋。
