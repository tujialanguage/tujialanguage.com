ENDongdongkui, dongdongkui. We went to Yongshun to play and to Baojing to kick shuttlecocks. We went to Longtou to drink water and climbed the slope at Jingdong. The Balie daughter-in-law is in postpartum confinement: first fry three eggs, then kill a rooster. Her maternal grandparents will visit, bringing a cradle, quilt, puppy, rice sweets, chicken, eggs and a pork leg.

ZH咚咚喹，咚咚喹。到永顺街上玩去了，到保靖街上踢毽子去了。哩哩拉拉咚咚喹，到隆头街上喝水去了，到井洞坡爬坡去了。咚咚喹，巴列媳子坐月子。她自己坐，先煎三个鸡蛋，再杀一个公鸡。明后天外公外婆要来看望，送摇篮、送被子，小狗要带来，糖糍、鸡、鸡蛋全都挑来，还有猪腿也要背来。

Cultural note · 文化注释

A children's rhyme built around the refrain ‘dongdongkui.’ Its journey through Yongshun, Baojing, Longtou and Jingdong anchors the song in familiar regional places.

这是一首以“咚咚喹”为衬词的童谣，歌词串联永顺、保靖、隆头、井洞等地方。
