DraftMar max niex biuf jief hor lair ar deiv jir, jiuf jiux niex biuf pux hir ceir.

IPA[ma²¹ ma⁵⁵ nie⁵⁵ piou³⁵ tɕie³⁵ xo²¹ lai²¹ a²¹ tei⁵³ tɕi²¹, tɕiou³⁵ tɕiou⁵⁵ nie⁵⁵ piou³⁵ pʰu⁵⁵ xi²¹ tsʰei²¹.]

ENAn aunt's daughter can be taken by reaching out; a maternal uncle's daughter cannot be afforded.

ZH姑姑女伸手取，舅舅女买不起。

Cultural note · 文化注释

This rhyme reflects an older Longshan marriage custom in which a man had a preferential claim to marry his paternal aunt's daughter. It contrasts the supposedly easy-to-obtain aunt's daughter with the highly valued maternal uncle's daughter.

这首顺口溜反映旧时龙山男子可优先娶姑姑之女的婚俗，以“姑姑女伸手取、舅舅女买不起”对比两种表亲婚配中的身份观念。

IPA[zuã³⁵ nie⁵⁵ lũ⁵⁵ ta⁵⁵ ta⁵⁵ tɕi⁵⁵; la⁵⁵ tsʰo⁵⁵ la⁵⁵ la⁵⁵ xuã⁵⁵ tɕi⁵⁵.]

ENTwo buffaloes fight and the whole family rejoices.—Pounding rice cakes

ZH两头水牛打架，全家欢喜。——打粑粑

Cultural note · 文化注释

Riddle answer: pounding rice. Two pestles are imagined as buffaloes fighting, while the work produces food for the whole household.

谜底是打粑粑，两根对杆被比作两头水牛打架，劳动成果让全家欢喜。

36

DraftNier maix wunx wunx mor zif; bif maix rav rav mor wunf.

IPA[nie²¹ mai⁵⁵ wũ⁵⁵ wũ⁵⁵ mo²¹ tsɿ³⁵; pi³⁵ mai⁵⁵ za⁵³ za⁵³ mo²¹ wũ³⁵.]

ENThe mother hums and cries; the child slowly grows.—Spinning yarn

ZH娘呢嗡嗡地哭，儿呢冉冉地长。——纺纱

Cultural note · 文化注释

Riddle answer: a spinning wheel. Its humming is likened to a mother, while the growing coil of yarn is likened to her child slowly growing.

谜底是纺车：纺车嗡嗡作响如母亲低吟，纱线越缠越大如孩子慢慢长大。
