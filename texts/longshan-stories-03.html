ENDuring the completion feast, people could not find the young mason. When they looked up, they saw him rising into the sky. They then understood that he was an immortal who had come down to help Peng Zepan build Qingkou Bridge.

ZH完工办圆工酒时，大家找不到小石匠，抬头一看，他正往天上升去。人们这才明白，他是下凡来帮彭泽畔修庆口桥的神仙。

Cultural note · 文化注释

An origin story for Qingkou Bridge. It remembers the difficulty of regional travel, communal labor, donor names carved on a stone stele and a mysterious young mason believed to be a deity who enabled the bridge to be completed.

这是庆口桥的地方传说，记录旧时往来龙山道路艰难、众人集资出工、石碑刻捐资者姓名，以及神秘小石匠下凡助建桥梁的故事。
