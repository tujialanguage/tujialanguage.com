DraftA: nif rix. B: nif rix-pier, rix luo. A: tianrdafbangv le, aix luov hairsif bixjiaox wof, yanfzix youf hangf zer-daf.

SourceA: ni2 ri1. B: ni2 ri1-pie3, ri1 luo. A: tian3da2bang4 le, ai1 luo4 hai3si2 bi1jiao1 wo2, yan2zi1 you2 hang2 ze3-da2.

ENA: You do it. B: You speak first. A: Tian Daban, he was a rather ferocious character, not so good-looking either.

ZHA：你说。B：你先说，说吧。A：田大板呢，那个人还是比较凶，样子又不太好看。

DraftDangfsif guof angfnix taxsax gaif huanfweir le, guof hairsif luanf gaox-daf, qingxmornier le? guof ri, gaif bengfdix gaof-cair hairsif dianfkuix liurqingr-duor-nief, suoxyix le, guof hairsif luanf gaox-tax,

SourceDang2si2 guo2 ang2ni1 ta1sa1 gai2 huan2wei3 le, guo2 hai3si2 luan2 gao1-da2, qing1mo3nie3 le? guo2 ri, gai2 beng2di1 gao2-cai3 hai3si2 dian2kui1 liu3qing3-duo3-nie2, suo1yi1 le, guo2 hai3si2 luan2 gao1-ta1,

ENBut to us in the Tasha area, he didn't cause any trouble. Why? Because he had to show some consideration for the local area. Therefore, he didn't cause trouble here.

ZH但是他在我们他砂这个范围呢，他还是不会乱来的。为什么呢？他呀，在本地这里还是要留一点情。所以呢，他还是不肯乱搞。

DraftDangfsif le, gaif luov le hairsif bixjiaox, xingx bixjiaox duf, guof rurguox six nif dianfkuix aixdix (null)-liaor le, guof jiux nif rix-alier-hur. luovdiex le bef guof gev.

SourceDang2si2 le, gai2 luo4 le hai3si2 bi1jiao1, xing1 bi1jiao1 du2, guo2 ru3guo1 si1 ni2 dian2kui1 ai1di1 (null)-liao3 le, guo2 jiu1 ni2 ri1-alie3-hu3. luo4die1 le be2 guo2 ge4.

ENHowever, this chap, he was pretty wicked. If you upset him at all, he would do away with you. All the folk, they were afraid of him.

ZH但是呢，这个人呢，心还是比较毒。如果是你得罪了他呢，他就会把你弄死。人家呢，也怕他。

DraftAix jiaxhuox zafsix ei! hei! qiev sirjier guof-jiaor sef-liaor duor haxtaif, a! gairdix jiaxhuox bufdiexliaox! suoxyix, guof angf gaof-cair six suanf bixjiaox... B: zef-lur wof-nier-xif luov laf hur. A: six bufsix hangfnier, nif youf rix.

SourceAi1 jia1huo1 za2si1 ei! hei! qie4 si3jie3 guo2-jiao3 se2-liao3 duo3 ha1tai2, a! gai3di1 jia1huo1 bu2die1liao1! suo1yi1, guo2 ang2 gao2-cai3 si1 suan2 bi1jiao1... B: ze2-lu3 wo2-nie3-xi2 luo4 la2 hu3. A: si1 bu2si1 hang2nie3, ni2 you2 ri1.

ENThat fellow was amazing! Hey! You could die at his hands without even knowing about it. Wow! That chap was really amazing! So, in our area he was reckoned to be rather... B: ... a fierce chap. A: Wasn't he like that? You say your bit as well.

ZH那家伙很厉害！嘿！哪时死在他手上都不会知道。啊！这个家伙真不得了！所以，他在我们这里算是比较...B：凶的一个人。A：是不是这样，你又讲吧。

DraftB: guof zengxzengf-di rix-sir-liaor, xingfdix jiux tianrzengxsuf lax hhir-langf, guof jiux six liurlangrdongf qievlir-lier, guof gaixhuanrjingx yirluofpair har-sir-lier.

SourceB: guo2 zeng1zeng2-di ri1-si3-liao3, xing2di1 jiu1 tian3zeng1su2 la1 hhi3-lang2, guo2 jiu1 si1 liu3lang3dong2 qie4li3-lie3, guo2 gai1huan3jing1 yi3luo2pai3 ha3-si3-lie3.

ENB: The only one he actually killed was Tian Zhengshu, from Xindi village, he went into Niulan Cave [to hide], and there he shot dead Yi Luopai from the People's Liberation Army,

ZHB：真正被他弄死的，就是信地村的田珍树，他就是进了牛栏洞，他把解放军的人打死了。

DraftHar-lier, dangxsir kuoxnengr zuif kuaif-le hairsif tianrqiaor weifjixwanf a, gev aix yif paox guof huxsiv, sarguox le gaixhuanrjingx guof zuof-duorliaor yixhouf le, kuoxbax-buflix tief-liaor.

ENhe shot him. Perhaps the first person to arrive there after the shooting was Wei Jiwan from Tianqiao Village; there was a group under his command. In the end, the People's Liberation Army caught him, and afterwards cut off his head.

ZH打了，当时可能最快了，还是天桥村姓卫的，他们那一群人服侍他。最后了，解放军把他抓到了，以后呢，砍头了。

DraftA: tief-lier hair karmongr gar suox niex guaf-lier ba. B: suox niex guaf-lier, qingrnief suox niex guaf-lier yixhouf le, guof kuoxbax-buflix yixrangr garhaf duor huor-lur la, eng, huor-lur.

SourceA: tie2-lie3 hai3 ka3mong3 ga3 suo1 nie1 gua2-lie3 ba. B: suo1 nie1 gua2-lie3, qing3nie2 suo1 nie1 gua2-lie3 yi1hou2 le, guo2 kuo1ba1-bu2li1 yi1rang3 ga3ha2 duo3 huo3-lu3 la, eng, huo3-lu3.

ENA: Cut it off and hung it on a tree for three days. B: Hung it up for three days, and after those three days, his head was taken away by the leaders.

ZHA：砍了还在树上挂了三天啦。B：挂了三天，后来挂了三天以后呢，他的头依然被领导拿去了。

DraftA: cixkixtax huor pex-lur-xif. B: eng, cixkixtax huor pex-lur-xif. A: aixdix zuifhouf, guof bongr-bor-xif aox-cair, darpiex six cer xief huof lir-lax o, qingrnief qingrmox, guof bongr-lier yixhouf le cer youf taof wa.

ENThey took it away. A: Took it off wrapped in paper. B: They wrapped it in paper. A: Then finally, the place where they buried him, it used to have a spring, then after he was buried, there was no longer any water.

ZH哼！拿去了。A：用纸包去的。B：拿纸包去的。A：那最后呢，听说，他被埋葬的那地方以前是有水的。后来，怎么他埋了以后呢，水又没有了。
