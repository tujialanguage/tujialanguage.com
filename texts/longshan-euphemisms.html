01

DraftSuanx dix

IPA[suã⁵⁵ ti⁵⁵]

ENPaired; an even number

ZH双的；双数。

Cultural note · 文化注释

Tujia people favor even numbers. Weddings are usually held on even-numbered dates, since good things are believed to come in pairs and double happiness enters the home. Cash gifts and presents often contain the number six, invoking the blessing ‘everything goes smoothly in sixes.’ Twelve dishes are prepared for New Year to symbolize prosperity in every month; four dishes are made on the first day of the lunar year to symbolize prosperity through all four seasons.

土家人喜欢双数，娶媳妇一般选择双数日子，认为好事成双，双喜临门。送礼金、买礼物喜欢带“六”的数字，有六就意味着六六大顺。过年做十二道菜，寓意月月发，正月初一做四道菜，寓意四季发财。

02

DraftJif cair

IPA[tɕi³⁵ tsʰai²¹]

ENBringing in firewood; a New Year expression for bringing in wealth

ZH进柴；正月初一背柴进屋，寓意新年进财。

Cultural note · 文化注释

On the first day of the lunar year, Tujia communities traditionally carry firewood home from the mountains. Bringing firewood into the house symbolizes wealth entering in the new year, because ‘firewood’ and ‘wealth’ are both pronounced [tsʰai²¹] in the local Chinese dialect.

农历正月初一，土家族地区有到山里背柴回家的习俗。初一背柴进屋寓意新年进财。当地汉语方言“柴”和“财”都读 [tsʰai²¹]。

03

DraftWunr bor daof

IPA[wũ²¹ po²¹ tau³⁵]

ENNo longer here; a euphemism meaning that someone has died

ZH没在了；委婉表示去世了。

Cultural note · 文化注释

This expression means that someone has died. [wũ²¹] means ‘sit,’ [po²¹] is a continuative particle, and [tau³⁵] is the perfective form of the negative word [ta⁵⁵].

去世了。[wũ²¹] 即“坐”，[po²¹] 为表持续态的助词，[tau³⁵] 即否定词 [ta⁵⁵] 的完成体。

04

DraftRof sir gaif

IPA[zo³⁵ sɿ²¹ kaɪ³⁵]

ENGoing to eat mutton; a euphemism for approaching death

ZH将要吃羊肉；委婉表示将要离世。

Cultural note · 文化注释

This euphemism means that someone is approaching death. [zo³⁵ sɿ²¹] means ‘mutton,’ and [kaɪ³⁵] is the prospective form of [ka³⁵] ‘eat.’ It derives from the Longshan Tujia custom of slaughtering a sheep for a funeral: saying that mutton will soon be eaten implies that someone is about to die.

（人）将要离世。[zo³⁵ sɿ²¹] 即“羊肉”，[kaɪ³⁵] 为 [ka³⁵]“吃”的将行体。这种委婉表达源于龙山土家族地区办丧事要宰羊的习俗，将要吃羊肉就意味着有人将离世。

05

DraftSunx koux hur

IPA[sũ⁵⁵ kʰoɪ⁵⁵ xu²¹]

ENGoing back soon; an elderly person's euphemism for approaching death

ZH将要回去了；老人委婉表示自己快要离世。

Cultural note · 文化注释

An elderly person uses this expression to say that they are nearing death. [sũ⁵⁵ kʰoɪ⁵⁵] is the prospective form of [sũ⁵⁵ kʰo⁵⁵] ‘go back,’ and [xu²¹] is a particle.

（老人）自己快要离世了。[sũ⁵⁵ kʰoɪ⁵⁵] 为 [sũ⁵⁵ kʰo⁵⁵]“回去”的将行体，[xu²¹] 为助词。

06

DraftKax jix lax

IPA[kʰa⁵⁵ tɕi⁵⁵ la⁵⁵]

ENHaving a hard time; to be ill

ZH不好过；生病。

Cultural note · 文化注释

The expression means ‘to be ill.’ A fuller expression, [xu⁵³ mũ²¹ tsʰa³⁵ ta⁵⁵], means ‘the whole body feels unwell.’ [kʰa⁵⁵] means ‘pass or endure,’ [tɕi⁵⁵ la⁵⁵] means ‘not good,’ [xu⁵³ mũ²¹] means ‘the whole body,’ [tsʰa³⁵] means ‘good,’ and [ta⁵⁵] is the negative ‘not.’

[xu⁵³ mũ²¹ tsʰa³⁵ ta⁵⁵] 浑身不舒服。生病。[kʰa⁵⁵] 即“过”，[tɕi⁵⁵ la⁵⁵] 即“不好”；[xu⁵³ mũ²¹] 即“浑身”，[tsʰa³⁵] 即“好”，[ta⁵⁵] 为否定词“不”。

08

DraftSir gof heiv liaov

IPA[sɿ²¹ ko³⁵ xei⁵³ liau⁵³]

ENBitten by a snake

ZH被蛇咬了。

Cultural note · 文化注释

This means ‘bitten by a snake.’ Literally the expression speaks of being ‘pricked by grass’: [sɿ²¹] means ‘grass,’ [ko³⁵] is a passive particle, [xei⁵³] means ‘prick,’ and [liau⁵³] is a completive particle.

被蛇咬了。[sɿ²¹] 即“草”，[ko³⁵] 为表被动的助词，[xei⁵³] 即“戳”，[liau⁵³] 为完成态助词。

09

DraftLox gor wunf qix lix liaox

IPA[lo⁵⁵ ko²¹ wũ³⁵ tɕʰi⁵⁵ li⁵⁵ liau⁵⁵]

ENSomeone pierced her nose; a euphemism for an unmarried woman having an affair

ZH别人把她鼻子穿了；委婉表示未婚女子与人私通。

Cultural note · 文化注释

This euphemism refers to an unmarried woman having a sexual relationship. [lo⁵⁵] means ‘another person,’ [ko²¹] means ‘she,’ [wũ³⁵ tɕʰi⁵⁵] means ‘nose,’ and [li⁵⁵] means ‘pierce.’

未婚女子与人私通。[lo⁵⁵] 即“别人”，[ko²¹] 即“她”，[wũ³⁵ tɕʰi⁵⁵] 即“鼻子”，[li⁵⁵] 即“穿”。

12

DraftCov wunr

IPA[tsʰo⁵³ wũ²¹]

ENSitting in the house; postpartum confinement

ZH坐到屋里；坐月子。

Cultural note · 文化注释

This means ‘to observe postpartum confinement.’ [tsʰo⁵³] means ‘house’ and [wũ²¹] means ‘sit.’ Another expression is [niã²¹ tsʰu³⁵ wũ²¹], ‘sit in a cool place’; [niã²¹ tsʰu³⁵] means a cool, shaded place.

坐月子。[tsʰo⁵³] 即“屋子”，[wũ²¹] 即“坐”。又说成 [niã²¹ tsʰu³⁵ wũ²¹]“坐到凉处”，[niã²¹ tsʰu³⁵] 即“凉处”，指阴凉的地方。

13

DraftRar sir gaf

IPA[za²¹ sɿ²¹ ka³⁵]

ENEating chicken; a euphemism for giving birth

ZH吃鸡肉；委婉表示生孩子。

Cultural note · 文化注释

This is a euphemism for giving birth. It is connected with the Tujia custom of a woman eating chicken during postpartum confinement.

生孩子。这种说法与土家族地区坐月子时产妇吃鸡肉的习俗有关。

14

DraftWaf kax cir laox tir

IPA[wua³⁵ kʰa⁵⁵ tsʰi²¹ lau⁵⁵ tʰi²¹]

ENGetting a cowherd; giving birth to a boy

ZH得一个守牛的；委婉表示生了一个男孩。

Cultural note · 文化注释

This means that a boy has been born. In rural life, having a son meant gaining a child who could herd cattle. Another expression is [ciã⁵⁵ lũ⁵⁵ po⁵⁵ po⁵⁵ lau⁵⁵ tʰi²¹]; [ciã⁵⁵ lũ⁵⁵ po⁵⁵ po⁵⁵] means ‘incense burner,’ so a son also represents someone who will continue the family line and ancestral rites.

生了一个男孩。对农村人而言，生男孩就意味着有了个放牛娃。又说成 [ciã⁵⁵ lũ⁵⁵ po⁵⁵ po⁵⁵ lau⁵⁵ tʰi²¹]，[ciã⁵⁵ lũ⁵⁵ po⁵⁵ po⁵⁵] 即香炉，生男孩也就意味着家里有了传承香火的人。

15

DraftReif tix kux laox tir

IPA[zei³⁵ tʰi⁵⁵ kʰu⁵⁵ lau⁵⁵ tʰi²¹]

ENGetting a wine jar; giving birth to a girl

ZH得一个酒坛子；委婉表示生了一个女孩。

Cultural note · 文化注释

This means that a girl has been born. It reflects the custom that a married daughter usually brings wine when visiting her parents; having a daughter therefore means that there will be wine to drink.

生了一个女孩。这种说法与土家族的习俗有关：已婚的女儿探望父母时大多会送酒，生了女儿就意味着有酒喝。
