1.4: The Story of the Eight Kings1.4: 八部大王的故事

This text relates the legend of the Eight Great Kings, brothers reputed to be the ancestors of the Tujia in northern Xiangxi. As well as describing the origins of the Tujia, the tale explains the significance of the dragon and phoenix totems in their traditional baishouwu dance. This is a prepared text, written down using Chinese characters as phonetic aide-memoires to indicate Tujia words. The photo archive includes a photograph of the footprint reputed to have been left on a large boulder by one of the eight brothers.

这是八部大王的故事，据土家族的传说，这八位兄弟是湘西北部土家族的祖先。除了描述土家族的族源之外，本故事也解释了龙和凤对于土家族人的重要性； 要在桅杆柱子顶上挂两面龙凤旗。这是田先生预先准备好的文本，在手稿中他使用了汉字来代表土家语词汇的发音。

DraftYertircixbax-nier zef lir.

SourceYe3ti3ci1ba1-nie3 ze2 li3.

ENThe Story of the Eight Kings

ZH八部大王的传说。

DraftYertircixbax bifzixkar zef lir-zuf-nier luovcixbax, bifzixkar-nier niertirgextix.

SourceYe3ti3ci1ba1 bi2zi1ka3 ze2 li3-zu2-nie3 luo4ci1ba1, bi2zi1ka3-nie3 nie3ti3ge1ti1.

ENThe Eight Kings are major characters in Tujia legends and are the ancestors of the Tujia people.

ZH八部大王是土家族传说中的大人物，是土家族的祖先。

DraftQieflongx-nier zixgex, pafnix porpax niex hur, rex hif longx niev sextongx tafcix-zuf, boxlir taif.

SourceQie2long1-nie3 zi1ge1, pa2ni1 po3pa1 nie1 hu3, re1 hi2 long1 nie4 se1tong1 ta2ci1-zu2, bo1li3 tai2.

ENOnce upon a time, there was an old couple who after more than forty years still remained childless.

ZH很久年前，两位公婆，四十多岁了还没有小孩。

DraftTaxniex jiefrix-hixcex, longv-max taif-xif gev, laf niex hivlier zif. eix laf niex, maxpar arsir-nier porpaxceix laov pafnix bor lir: "nif guof engxdix rafgux-buflix yer buf luof mor cer hur-lier qingfdiex xief."

SourceTa1nie1 jie2ri1-hi1ce1, long4-ma1 tai2-xi2 ge4, la2 nie1 hi4lie3 zi2. ei1 la2 nie1, ma1pa3 a3si3-nie3 po3pa1cei1 lao4 pa2ni1 bo3 li3: "ni2 guo2 eng1di1 ra2gu1-bu2li1 ye3 bu2 luo2 mo3 ce3 hu3-lie3 qing2die1 xie2."

ENEvery day they wept, afraid that when they were too old to work there would be no one to look after them. One day a white-bearded old man said to the old woman: "Take these eight tea-leaves, drink the tea brewed from them, and then you will have children."

ZH以后做不起工夫时怕没人瞻养，就天天哭泣。有一天，一位白胡子老头对老婆婆说：“你把这八颗茶叶籽籽熬水吃了，就会有喜。”

DraftPafnix guof-jiaor-liex rafgux-buflix yer buf huor-lier cux luof mor cer hur-lier, suox longx wor six yev-nier langrceix boxlir longv-liaor.

SourcePa2ni1 guo2-jiao3-lie1 ra2gu1-bu2li1 ye3 bu2 huo3-lie3 cu1 luo2 mo3 ce3 hu3-lie3, suo1 long1 wo3 si1 ye4-nie3 lang3cei1 bo1li3 long4-liao3.

ENThe old lady took the eight tea-leaves home with her, made tea from them and drank it. One evening, after three and a half years, she gave birth.

ZH老婆从他手中把八颗茶叶籽籽拿回家熬水吃了。过了三年六个月，就在那天晚上生了孩子。

DraftPorpaxceix xiexhux congrgaor tievtier tif-bor bav-xief: "mef! boxlir heflar! boxlir qianflafbux!" laov laov mor hiv-lier, yer hur-nier luovbir!

SourcePo3pa1cei1 xie1hu1 cong3gao3 tie4tie3 ti2-bo3 ba4-xie2: "me2! bo1li3 he2la3! bo1li3 qian2la2bu1!" lao4 lao4 mo3 hi4-lie3, ye3 hu3-nie3 luo4bi3!

ENHer husband immediately lit an oil-lamp to have a look. Good heavens! Such children, so many of them! Counting them one by one, altogether there were eight boys!

ZH老头子立刻点着丛膏油灯去看。天啊！孩子，这么多的孩子！一个个数起来有八个男孩啊！

DraftPorpaxceix gev-xirtaif, qiev diefkaxlax laf tongv dief-lier, guof guof rongxtix laf rongx huorlier wov-lier kangrkux kuxzav gar-bor tuf-lur.

SourcePo3pa1cei1 ge4-xi3tai2, qie4 die2ka1la1 la2 tong4 die2-lie3, guo2 guo2 rong1ti1 la2 rong1 huo3lie3 wo4-lie3 kang3ku1 ku1za4 ga3-bo3 tu2-lu3.

ENThe old man was very frightened, wondering what kind of evil brood they might be. He carried them away in a large back-basket and abandoned them in the mountains.

ZH老头子怕得很，认为是一窝怪物。他用一个大背笼背到山上丢弃了。

DraftLuovbir yer laxhur guafguaf mor zif-zuf, lif arsir laov engrzouf, zafqix gafgax mor boxlir gaf-zuf, pur tiefbax laov raf-diuf, jieftangxkex huorlier boxlir-diex afhux-bor, lif arsir gev mor xivcar-lur.

SourceLuo4bi3 ye3 la1hu3 gua2gua2 mo3 zi2-zu2, li2 a3si3 lao4 eng3zou2, za2qi1 ga2ga1 mo3 bo1li3 ga2-zu2, pu3 tie2ba1 lao4 ra2-diu2, jie2tang1ke1 huo3lie3 bo1li3-die1 a2hu1-bo3, li2 a3si3 ge4 mo3 xi4ca3-lu3.

ENWhilst the eight babies were crying, a white tiger appeared. Just as it was opening its mouth wide to eat them, a big phoenix came flying down and used its wings to protect the children. The tiger ran away in fright.

ZH八个男孩哇哇地哭时，一只白虎来了。张开嘴巴要吃小孩的时候，一只大凤凰飞来，用翅膀护着小孩们，白虎怕得跑了。

DraftTaxniex, puf tiefbax laov engrzouf, puf, guof boxlir-diex bor mangr av; huanr, guof boxlir-diex bor suovtir luov.

SourceTa1nie1, pu2 tie2ba1 lao4 eng3zou2, pu2, guo2 bo1li3-die1 bo3 mang3 a4; huan3, guo2 bo1li3-die1 bo3 suo4ti3 luo4.

ENLater, a large dragon also came. The dragon suckled the children and the phoenix warmed them with its body.

ZH后来，又来了一条大龙。龙给孩子们喂奶，凤凰给孩子们暖身体。

DraftHangflar, boxlir yer laxhur puf-nier mangr kur, pur-nier luov-bor ongf-lier luovcixbax zif-liaor.

SourceHang2la3, bo1li3 ye3 la1hu3 pu2-nie3 mang3 ku3, pu3-nie3 luo4-bo3 ong2-lie3 luo4ci1ba1 zi2-liao3.

ENSo, drinking the dragon's milk and warmed by the phoenix, the eight children grew to adulthood.

ZH这样，八个孩子哺龙乳暖凤温长大成人了。

DraftEix laf niex, yer laxhur luovbir-diex daxbur mor gercif-lax, puf niex pur gevzex bor lir: "sef tav gercif-guf, xiexhux nierbar bav-i-duor-hur, nierbar sef dief mor zif mor luofbuxtafpav duor hur-liaor."

SourceEi1 la2 nie1, ye3 la1hu3 luo4bi3-die1 da1bu3 mo3 ge3ci2-la1, pu2 nie1 pu3 ge4ze1 bo3 li3: "se2 ta4 ge3ci2-gu2, xie1hu1 nie3ba3 ba4-i-duo3-hu3, nie3ba3 se2 die2 mo3 zi2 mo3 luo2bu1ta2pa4 duo3 hu3-liao3."

ENOne day, when the eight young men were enjoying themselves wrestling with one another, the dragon and the phoenix said to them: "Don't play any more, go quickly and visit your parents, their eyes are swollen with crying from missing you."

ZH有一天，八个青年正在摔跤玩耍，龙和凤对他们说：“你们别再玩耍了，快看你们父母去。你们父母想你们哭得眼皮都肿了。”

DraftLuovbir yer laxhur kuxzav dar-lier xivcar mor cux-nier nierbar yir-liaor, kiv-bor mor axnier arbax jier.

SourceLuo4bi3 ye3 la1hu3 ku1za4 da3-lie3 xi4ca3 mo3 cu1-nie3 nie3ba3 yi3-liao3, ki4-bo3 mo3 a1nie3 a3ba1 jie3.

ENThe eight boys ran down the mountain to their parents' home. When they saw them, they knelt down and cried, "Father! Mother!"

ZH八个男儿下山跑到家见了父母，跪着喊父亲母亲。

DraftNierbar, guof luovbir-diex songxkuox-xif yir-lier, niev mor luofbuxcer safgix duor-diuf.

SourceNie3ba3, guo2 luo4bi3-die1 song1kuo1-xi2 yi3-lie3, nie4 mo3 luo2bu1ce3 sa2gi1 duo3-diu2.

ENWhen their parents saw they had returned, they cried for joy.

ZH父母见儿子们回来了，笑得流出了热泪。

DraftAxnier arbax gevzex luov tiefbax bifkuix laov wa jier-xif laov jier: ngaorcerhuorsev, xixtixlaov, xixaxlaov, lixdux, suxdux, laxwuxmix, longxcixyexsuoxyexcongx, jiefyexhuixyexlaxhuixliexyex.

SourceA1nie3 a3ba1 ge4ze1 luo4 tie2ba1 bi2kui1 lao4 wa jie3-xi2 lao4 jie3: ngao3ce3huo3se4, xi1ti1lao4, xi1a1lao4, li1du1, su1du1, la1wu1mi1, long1ci1ye1suo1ye1cong1, jie2ye1hui1ye1la1hui1lie1ye1.

ENThey then gave each of them names, from the eldest to the youngest: Ngaocehuose, Xitilao, Xi'alao, Lidu, Sudu, Lawumi, Longciyesuoyecong, and Jieyehuiyelahuilieye.

ZH父母就给他们从大到小起了名字：熬泽河舍、西梯佬、西阿佬、里都、苏都、那乌米、拢此也所也冲、接也会也那飞列也。

DraftGevzex jier-nier-xif tir-alier, luov guof yer hur qianxngaiv-diex jier.

SourceGe4ze1 jie3-nie3-xi2 ti3-alie3, luo4 guo2 ye3 hu3 qian1ngai4-die1 jie3.

ENAfter they had been given their names, people called them the Eight Brothers.

ZH他们得了名字以后，人们称他们为八兄弟。

DraftYer hur qianxngaiv-diex cux jiefrix, tuofgax arbur dir, kangrkux sirjiev, hurpar songf zuof, laf yer hixhix rix-xif caf;

SourceYe3 hu3 qian1ngai4-die1 cu1 jie2ri1, tuo2ga1 a3bu3 di3, kang3ku1 si3jie4, hu3pa3 song2 zuo2, la2 ye3 hi1hi1 ri1-xi2 ca2;

ENThe eight brothers worked around the home, digging bracken and kudzu, hunting in the hills, catching fish in the rivers; whatever they did, they did well.

ZH八兄弟在家务农，挖蕨打葛，上山打猎，下河捉鱼，样样工夫都做得好。

DraftGevzex xiexqix caf-xirtaif, merguor woftux zer, wuxnirzir caf, kangrkux kar wov, karmongr cixbax laf mongx laf mongx mor bev-adif-dixxir, kangrkux sirjiev, lif tiefbax jief huorlier bixciv mor niex zax zif.

SourceGe4ze1 xie1qi1 ca2-xi3tai2, me3guo3 wo2tu1 ze3, wu1ni3zi3 ca2, kang3ku1 ka3 wo4, ka3mong3 ci1ba1 la2 mong1 la2 mong1 mo3 be4-adi2-di1xi3, kang3ku1 si3jie4, li2 tie2ba1 jie2 huo3lie3 bi1ci4 mo3 nie1 za1 zi2.

ENThey were full of might, both brave and resourceful. When they went into the mountains to fetch firewood, they could bring back a whole tree at a time; when they went hunting, they could tear a big tiger in two with their bare hands.

ZH他们力大无穷，智勇双全，上山背柴，可以把大树一棵一棵地搬起来，上山打猎，用手把大老虎掰开两半。

DraftGevzex yer hur qianxngaiv-diex laf max nievkax-xif caf-liaor, luov davxiv mor, hufnix nievkax caf-liaor.

SourceGe4ze1 ye3 hu3 qian1ngai4-die1 la2 ma1 nie4ka1-xi2 ca2-liao3, luo4 da4xi4 mo3, hu2ni1 nie4ka1 ca2-liao3.

ENThe eight brothers and their parents lived happily together, and helped others to live happily too.

ZH他们八兄弟全家日子过得好了，又帮助大家过上好日子。

DraftEix laf longx, wovtar-nier luovdiex angf guorjiax har-diuf, huanrdif guof har mor hhif-yaxtix, sengfzix dar-lier yer hur qianxngaiv-diex duor [har-xief].

SourceEi1 la2 long1, wo4ta3-nie3 luo4die1 ang2 guo3jia1 ha3-diu2, huan3di2 guo2 ha3 mo3 hhi2-ya1ti1, seng2zi1 da3-lie3 ye3 hu3 qian1ngai4-die1 duo3 [ha3-xie2].

ENOne year, foreigners came to attack our country. The emperor was unable to repulse them, so he issued an edict ordering the eight brothers to go and fight.

ZH有一年，外面的人来攻打我们国家。皇帝打不退敌人，就下圣旨叫八兄弟一起去打。

DraftYer hur qianxngaiv-diex davhar-nier zixgix yev-lier, jief niex cir huorlier luov zuof-bor, laxsaxdiex kuoxbax-buflix tief-lur, laxsaxdiex jir niex cir cixcav-lier niex zax zif, luovcixbax guof carcaxkuxlix zuof-xif davzer, luov diefkaxlax qianflafbux har-sir.

SourceYe3 hu3 qian1ngai4-die1 da4ha3-nie3 zi1gi1 ye4-lie3, jie2 nie1 ci3 huo3lie3 luo4 zuo2-bo3, la1sa1die1 kuo1ba1-bu2li1 tie2-lu3, la1sa1die1 ji3 nie1 ci3 ci1ca4-lie3 nie1 za1 zi2, luo4ci1ba1 guo2 ca3ca1ku1li1 zuo2-xi2 da4ze3, luo4 die2ka1la1 qian2la2bu1 ha3-si3.

ENWhen the eight brothers reached the battlefield, they used their hands to seize the enemy: they plucked off the heads of some and tore the legs off others, just like a man grasping a grasshopper. They put to death many of the enemy.

ZH八兄弟到了打仗的前线，用两只手抓住敌人，有些人被摘掉了头，有些人的两只脚被扯成两半，好像大人捉蝗虫一样，打死了许多敌人。

DraftSef-daf-nier luovdiex kuoxbax afhux mor xivcar-jif-lur.

SourceSe2-da2-nie3 luo4die1 kuo1ba1 a2hu1 mo3 xi4ca3-ji2-lu3.

ENThose who survived fled, clutching their heads.

ZH没死的敌人抱着头逃跑了。

DraftYer hur qianxngaiv-diex guorjiax afhux mor gongx tir-lier, huanrdif, guof laov bor wanr laov pox, yertircixbax jier, laf hur laf dongf kav, laf dongf laf bufluov.

SourceYe3 hu3 qian1ngai4-die1 guo3jia1 a2hu1 mo3 gong1 ti3-lie3, huan3di2, guo2 lao4 bo3 wan3 lao4 po1, ye3ti3ci1ba1 jie3, la2 hu3 la2 dong2 ka4, la2 dong2 la2 bu2luo4.

ENThe eight brothers made a great contribution to saving the nation, so the emperor conferred a kingship on each of them, and called them the Eight Kings. He gave each of them a tract of land to rule, one per tribe.

ZH八兄弟保国立了功，皇帝给每人封了个王，叫八部大王，每人管一峒，一峒为一部落。

DraftZef lir, tiefbax longrcex ongr, bifkuix souxbaxdongx ongr.

SourceZe2 li3, tie2ba1 long3ce1 ong3, bi2kui1 sou1ba1dong1 ong3.

ENAccording to legend, the eldest ruled in Nongche [in Longshan] and the youngest in Shoubadong [in Baojing].

ZH传说，老大坐农车（龙山），老幺坐首八峒（保靖）。

DraftTaxniex-nier revbif-diex guof dief-zuf, sexbax rix-zuf gevzex jingf-lax.

SourceTa1nie1-nie3 re4bi2-die1 guo2 die2-zu2, se1ba1 ri1-zu2 ge4ze1 jing2-la1.

ENAfterwards, their descendants wanted to commemorate them, venerating them during the 'baishouwu' dance.

ZH后来的子孙们纪念他们时，跳着摆手舞敬他们。

DraftMuflax, baoxjingr suixbaxdongx yertircixbax-nier miaof tifmix aivduor xief-nief, longrsangx longrcex-nier sexbax cixbax rix-nier-xif luov hufnix rix-dexxir.

SourceMu2la1, bao1jing3 sui1ba1dong1 ye3ti3ci1ba1-nie3 miao2 ti2mi1 ai4duo3 xie2-nie2, long3sang1 long3ce1-nie3 se1ba1 ci1ba1 ri1-nie3-xi2 luo4 hu2ni1 ri1-de1xi3.

ENToday, the remains of the Eight Kings Temple still exist at Shuibadong in Baojing; and in Nongche in Longshan, people still know how to perform the large-scale 'baishouwu' dance.

ZH现在，保靖水坝峒八部大王庙的遗址还在，龙山农车的大摆手大家都会。

DraftZef lir-zuf, eix laf artongx gar yertircixbax-nier jirmif laov xief.

SourceZe2 li3-zu2, ei1 la2 a3tong1 ga3 ye3ti3ci1ba1-nie3 ji3mi2 lao4 xie2.

ENAccording to legend, one of the king's footprints can be seen on a depression in the rock there.

ZH传说时，那里在石头孔上面有一个八部大王的脚印。

DraftBifzixkar sexbax cixbax rix-zuf, weirzir kartongr garhaf puf niex pur qir niex hhix zuox-bor-duor, puf pur, guof bifzixkar-nier niertirgextix longv-nier qingr dief-xif.

SourceBi2zi1ka3 se1ba1 ci1ba1 ri1-zu2, wei3zi3 ka3tong3 ga3ha2 pu2 nie1 pu3 qi3 nie1 hhi1 zuo1-bo3-duo3, pu2 pu3, guo2 bi2zi1ka3-nie3 nie3ti3ge1ti1 long4-nie3 qing3 die2-xi2.

ENWhen the Tujia perform the large-scale 'baishouwu' dance, they must hang flags depicting a dragon and a phoenix on two poles, to commemorate the kindness shown by the dragon and phoenix in raising the ancestors of the Tujia.

ZH土家族跳大摆手舞时，要在桅杆柱子顶上挂两面龙凤旗，为了纪念龙凤扶养土家族远祖祖先的恩德。
