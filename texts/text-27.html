2.7.1: Attacked by Tufei Bandits2.7.1: 遇到土匪

This is a selection of texts drawn from Mrs Tian Xintao's memories of her early life in the Yongshun area prior to Liberation in 1949. The first two texts illustrate the rigours of life, at the hands of both men and nature. The third text provides a brief vignette of a traditional Tujia woman, accomplished in the economies of daily life. The final text describes the journey home from Changsha, the provincial capital of Hunan, to Yongshun, an arduous trip of over two hundred miles. Transport in the area was very limited at that time, and rivers were important arteries of communication.

ZH我的祖父母也很怕，我的父母很怕；我的小妹妹不怕。

2.7.2: Accidents in the Mountains2.7.2: 上山时发生的意外事件

DraftNgaf qingfdiex bif sir, ngaf-nier arbax ngaf jier-bor mor: "nif kangrkux hhif-duor, kar wov-duor, zivxir wuv-duor a! wufxir suov-duor a!", laf zef laf zef ngaf bor afyix.

ENWhen I was small, my grandmother called to me: "Go up into the hills and fetch some firewood, You also need to get some weeds for the pigs, and cut some grass for the cows!" So she lectured me, one sentence after another.

ZH我小的时候，我的祖母喊我：“你要去山里背柴。要扯猪草呀！要切牛草呀！”，一句一句地教我。

DraftNgaf-nier marmax le, guof ngaf davhar le, kangrkux-bor hhif ao, kar wov-i. angfnix eix-zuf kar wov, yifxix gar kar wov, gar kar wov-lier bar gur-lier.

SourceNga2-nie3 ma3ma1 le, guo2 nga2 da4ha3 le, kang3ku1-bo3 hhi2 ao, ka3 wo4-i. ang2ni1 ei1-zu2 ka3 wo4, yi2xi1 ga3 ka3 wo4, ga3 ka3 wo4-lie3 ba3 gu3-lie3.

ENSo my aunt, she went with me to the hills, to fetch firewood. So there we were collecting firewood, our backs laden with dry wood. With that on our backs, up the hill we went.

ZH我的姑母呢，她和我一起去山里去，去背柴。我们那时背柴，背的都是干柴，背干柴，要爬山。

ZH我爬到树上，上呀上呀，树倒下来了！哎哟！我和树一起倒下来，一句话都不能说。

DraftNgaf-nier marmax jier a, jier a: "kangrkux lix sef la! gengxkev-lier sef la! qingrmox rix a? qingrmox rix a?" guof jif-bor mor jier, jif-bor mor jier, jier-lier,

ZH我的姑母喊：“山里死人啦！滚死了！怎么办？怎么办？”她哭着喊，哭着喊，喊了。

ZH哎哟！他们爬山了，抱着我，抱着我，我还是不能说话，抱回来了。

ZH她的碗呢，她不肯洗碗。为什么？因为还有油和盐。
