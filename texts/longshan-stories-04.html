Cultural note · 文化注释

A Longshan Tujia flood-and-origin myth. The siblings Luo Jifu and Luo Jilian survive in a gourd, become the ancestors of renewed humanity, and create surnames through wordplay: embankment/Kang, bamboo/Zhu, field/Tian, shed/Peng, river/He and ‘finished’/Wang. The story also explains clothing customs such as headscarves, earrings and shoes.

这是一则龙山土家族洪水与人类起源神话。罗吉富、罗吉莲兄妹乘葫芦避过洪水，成为再造人类的祖先；故事又用坎—康、竹—朱、田—田、茅棚—彭、河—何、完—王等谐音解释姓氏来源，并解释头帕、耳环、鞋等穿戴习俗。
