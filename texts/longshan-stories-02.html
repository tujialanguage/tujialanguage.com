Cultural note · 文化注释

A local trickster tale. Chen Erlang wins a wager by writing an invitation to ‘Chen Erlang’ on his clothing, so passers-by read the words aloud and his brother mistakes this for people greeting him.

这是一则地方机智故事。陈二郎把请他坐下喝茶的话写在衣服上，路人读出文字，哥哥误以为人人都在喊陈二郎，因而输掉赌约。
