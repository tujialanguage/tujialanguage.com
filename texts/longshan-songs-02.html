Fine weather and rain天晴下雨

DraftLaor wuar cir, meif ax zeir. tiex bix lax kux ax seir? ngax gox zof mor liv qier! tiaor zir lar kox bier. lanf kux lax ax bier. tuanr qianr dier liaor. wuf bix lif lax wof tux peir liaor.

ENThe sun has come out; rain has fallen. Who is on the road opposite? Let me catch him and make him plough. Bend the cattle switch, join the yoke—but the connecting loop breaks, and the calf lies down in the furrow.

ZH出太阳了，下雨了。对面路上是谁呀？我把他捉过来耕地吧！先把赶牛的细棍子折弯，把牛轭连接起来；但团圈断了，小牛就在犁路里趴下了。

Cultural note · 文化注释

A playful work song in which changes between sunshine and rain frame scenes of ploughing, handling cattle and field labor.

这是一首带有劳动生活色彩的童谣，以天晴下雨为引子，写耕地、赶牛和田间劳作。
